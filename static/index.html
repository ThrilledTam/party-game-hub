Every team needs at least one player/bot. Anyone with no team is "Alone" — their own one-person team. Team score = the average of its members' scores.

Medium: + − × ÷, multiplication up to 12×12.

Secret Word: 1 impostor (4–5 players) or 2 (6–8). Everyone else sees the same secret word; the impostor must blend in. One clue each, then vote out the impostor — catch one and the word team wins.

President: shed all your cards first to become President. Play a set of the same rank that beats the trick, or pass. The 3 of clubs leads. Last player left is the Asshole.

UNO: match the top card by colour, number or symbol. Wilds let you pick the next colour. First to empty your hand wins.

Memory: memorize a 4-digit number, then type it back. Wrong and you're out! Survive and the number grows: 5 digits, 6, 7, 8… until one player remains. Recall time is 15 seconds.

Elimination: a series of quick challenges (Reaction, Speed Math, Speed Trivia, Memory). Last place in each challenge is eliminated, until 3 remain — then a finale crowns the victor. At the end, everyone votes Play Again or Return to Lobby.
